# Design Lab

Design Lab has three parts:

## 1. Design Lab website
A website that shares information about Design Lab and lets customers download the software to install on their own server.

## 2. Design Lab company dashboard
A private dashboard for us at Design Lab to manage the company.

## 3. Design Lab app
The app people use on their iPads, tablets and phones.

These are three parts of the same Design Lab product. The features, connection between them, and launch plan can be worked out step by step.
