Your files, arranged

Install Design Lab and the assistant arranges your project files, new and old. Bots trained on Design Lab's folders do the sorting.

  1. Bring in old projects

    First day

    The assistant asks where to keep your Design Lab folders, then whether to bring in your old project folders. They are copied and sorted. Your originals are never moved or changed.

  2. The Inbox sorts itself

    Every minute

    Drop any file into a project's 00 Inbox. Drawings, renders, site photos, MOMs and bills go to the right folder, and older drawing revisions move to Superseded.

  3. Smart Sorting Bot

    Uses AI

    Files with unclear names go to a bot trained on Design Lab's folders. It is paid per use from your prepaid wallet, and only when you switch it on.

  4. Nothing is lost

    Always

    Nothing is deleted. Removed files wait in the Bin for 30 days, and every sort can be undone with one button.

Good to know

Plain answers to what studios ask first.

Where are my files kept?

On your own computer, in folders you choose (or on Google Drive or OneDrive). Design Lab does not upload your project files anywhere.

Will it change my old folders?

No. Old project folders are copied in and sorted. The originals stay exactly as they were.

How do I use it on my phone?

Keep the computer on with Design Lab open. Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi, open Office, Today, Open on your phone, scan the code and sign in. No app to install.

Do I need the internet?

Day-to-day work runs on your computer. The AI bots and The Courtyard need the internet.

Is the payment real?

Not during the pilot. Registration shows a test payment and no money moves.

Windows says it protected my PC

That message appears for new software. Click More info, then Run anyway. If the firewall asks, click Allow so your phone can reach Design Lab.
